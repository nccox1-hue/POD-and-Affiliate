## Cross-Promotion Strategy

Traffic flows **from the affiliate site to POD shops** — not the other way round. eBay and Etsy policies prohibit external links in listings.

| What | How | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Gift guide articles | Write "Best funny cat gifts" etc. on affiliate site → link to NickPrintCo Etsy listings | Drives organic Google traffic directly to Etsy shop |
| eBay shop | Same — affiliate articles link to eBay listings for relevant products | Two sales channels benefiting from one content piece |
| Etsy/eBay shop profile | One external link allowed in shop bio — link to affiliate site homepage | Low volume but legitimate |
| "Made with AI" content angle | Articles about using AI for Etsy/POD businesses — naturally links to NickPrintCo as a live example | Credibility + traffic |

**Practical rule:** any article about gifts, novelty products, or print-on-demand should include a section linking to the Etsy/eBay shops. One site feeds the other.

## Monetisation Model

1. **Software affiliate commissions** — primary income. Recurring model means one good referral compounds monthly.
2. **Amazon Associates** — supplementary. Books, accessories, physical productivity tools.
3. **Udemy courses** — supplementary. Excel, Python, AI, automation courses.
4. **POD shop traffic** — indirect income. Affiliate articles drive buyers to Etsy/eBay listings.

**Realistic income timeline:**
- Months 1–3: £0 (building catalogue, SEO lag)
- Months 4–6: first commissions (£10–£50/month if 20+ articles live)
- Months 7–12: compounding (£50–£300/month with 50+ articles and rankings)

SEO takes 3–6 months to compound. Start early, publish consistently, do not expect fast returns.

## Legal Requirements (UK)

Every article that contains affiliate links **must** include a clear disclosure at the top. Required by ASA/CAP code — no exceptions.

Standard disclosure:

> *This article contains affiliate links. If you purchase through one of these links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.*
