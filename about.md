## About AutomateWork

AutomateWork publishes practical guides for business professionals who want to spend less time on repetitive tasks and more time on work that actually matters.

The guides focus on tools you already have access to — Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power Automate, and Power BI — and show you exactly how to apply them to real office workflows: approvals, reporting, data cleanup, notifications, and more.

### Who writes this?

The guides are written with a background in technical operations and process automation across the automotive and engineering sectors. The focus is always on practical, tested solutions rather than theoretical overviews.
