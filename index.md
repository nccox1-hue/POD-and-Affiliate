## Work smarter with Excel, Power Automate and Power BI

Every week, millions of office workers repeat the same manual tasks — copying data between spreadsheets, chasing approvals over email, building reports by hand. Most of these can be fully automated in an afternoon.

This site covers practical, step-by-step guides for automating real business workflows using the tools most companies already own: **Microsoft Excel**, **Power Query**, **Power Automate**, and **Power BI**. No coding background required.

---

### What you'll find here

- **Excel & Power Query** — clean, transform and combine data without VLOOKUP or manual copy-paste
- **Power Automate** — build automated workflows that run without you: approvals, notifications, data syncs
- **Power BI** — connect your data and build dashboards that update automatically
- **Tool comparisons** — honest comparisons of automation tools (Zapier vs Make, Power Automate vs Zapier, and more)
